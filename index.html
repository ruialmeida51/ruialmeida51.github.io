01 / About

An engineer who likes to understand things.

I have a degree in Software Engineering and I try my best to live up to the title. I've been writing Android apps since 2019, and since 2025 I've been going deep on Kotlin Multiplatform: shared logic, native UI where it matters.

I like owning my features end to end, working closely with product and design, and leaving codebases a bit more modern and testable than I found them. I also mentor graduates, which is one of the best parts of the job: explaining something out loud is the fastest way to find out whether you actually understand it.

Away from work I tinker with everything: computers I've taken apart far too many times, home servers, open-source side projects. Curiosity is the common thread.

7+years building Android apps

05 / AI

Using AI without my brain turning to paste.

I use AI every day and I like it. I also don't want to forget how to think. So I run it with some rules.

  1. Paper firstI handwrite what I want to build. No screen, no autocomplete.
  2. Rough draftA quick throwaway sketch in code to see if the idea is even feasible.
  3. Get roastedI talk it through with an AI and ask it to tear my solution apart.
  4. IterateRinse and repeat until I'm happy with every detail.
  5. ImplementOnly now does the AI write it, usually in one go, because the thinking is done.

For the complex stuff

I plan with a stronger model (Opus) and execute with a faster one (Sonnet). At work that happens through Copilot; on side projects and other Mindera work I use Claude. My own repos carry written instructions for the AI, with separate product, planner and implementer roles, so it works the way I would.

For the tiny stuff

Flipping a feature toggle, fixing a small bug: I let the AI rip on auto mode. Not everything deserves a ceremony.

The paper is the point. It keeps my brain sharp while the machine does the typing.

04 / The rules

How it works.

  1. A scenario every weekday. A small engineering decision: sync, retries, architecture trade-offs, KMP boundaries, or how Kotlin and Compose work inside.
  2. I answer from my head. Two or three lines, no AI, no lookups. Before I see anything I rate how sure I am, from 1 to 5.
  3. An AI reviews my reasoning, not just my conclusion, and the result is logged here as right, partly or missed.
  4. Wednesdays are no-AI days. Tickets by hand, then a lab where I predict, build, verify and break something from the inside. Every second week there's a design kata on paper.
  5. XP and levels: a decision is worth 10 XP, a lab or kata 30. Right earns full XP, partly 60%, missed 30%, because a miss I understand afterwards still teaches me something. Levels follow the RuneScape curve, so early ones fly by and level 99 in a skill takes 2,401 XP. Add all seven skills up for a total level, which unlocks titles.

Credit where it's due: the No-AI day is my Wednesday take on No AI Fridays, the weekly AI-free workday the htmx CEO mandated and put out to the world. The reason I bother comes from Anthropic's own study on how AI assistance affects learning to code.

Only dates, topics, confidence ratings and results appear here. Nothing from work, ever.
